Keep your account safe

  • Use a strong password that you do not reuse on other sites.
  • Never share your login details or any code you receive by SMS.
  • Log out on shared phones, and do not save your password on a device you do not own.
  • Only log in through the real Kaya4U platform. Be careful with links sent by strangers.

Responsible gambling

You must be 18 or over. Papua New Guinea has no domestic licensing regime for online casinos, so check the rules that apply to you. Set a budget before you play, take breaks, never chase losses, and stop if it stops being fun.
